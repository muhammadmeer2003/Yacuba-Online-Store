Store policies

Delivery, Returns, Privacy & Terms

Delivery

We deliver in the areas listed at checkout. The delivery fee for your area is shown before you place your order. Pickup from the store is free. Delivery time depends on your location and order volume; we will contact you to confirm.

Payment

Payment is by Cash on Delivery or Cash on Pickup unless we tell you otherwise.

Returns & Refunds

Because many items are frozen or fresh, we cannot accept returns for change of mind. If an item is damaged, spoiled, or different from your order, contact us on the same day with a photo and we will replace it or refund it.

Items sold by weight

Prices for weighed items (such as meat) are per kilogram. The actual weight may differ slightly after cutting and packing; the final amount is based on the actual weight.

Privacy

We collect your name, phone number and address only to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information. Contact us if you want your details removed.

Terms

Prices and availability can change without notice. By placing an order you confirm that the details you gave are correct. We may cancel an order that cannot be fulfilled.

Questions? Contact us using the details in the footer.
